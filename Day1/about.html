About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application for capturing and organizing your ideas and reminders. It helps you quickly save important notes so you can asily find and review them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and go to the Add a note section.
  2. Type your idea or reminder in the note box.
  3. Click the Add Note button to save your note.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • Keep your notes organized in one place.
  • Access your notes easily whenever you need them.
